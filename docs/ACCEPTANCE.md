# Live workflow check

1. Start dictation with the existing shortcut. Confirm the recorder and timer appear at the exact center of the screen containing the pointer, and the tall left-hand voice bars follow your voice and the surrounding badges act only as indicators.
2. With Transcription Streaming enabled, confirm words appear while you are still speaking. Recent words may change as more audio arrives. Continue for several sentences: earlier words should remain, and the panel should expand to show them on the right.
3. Stop. Confirm a final pass runs, the complete text is copied, a brief check/glow appears, and the view disappears automatically. Longer text gets a little more scan time.
4. Press ⌘V in a text document. Confirm the final transcript appears exactly once. The confirmation should disappear immediately when the existing keyboard listener receives ⌘V. No Accessibility prompt or required paste confirmation should appear; without keyboard-event access or when using menu paste, the timed dismissal remains.
5. Start another recording before the previous confirmation disappears. Confirm the old dismissal does not hide the new recording.
6. Disable Transcription Streaming in Preferences, then record again. Confirm the voice display/timer remain, no live text appears, and the final transcript still copies. Quit/reopen and verify the toggle remains off, then turn it back on if desired.
7. Cancel a recording. Confirm it stops and microphone input volume is restored when boosting is enabled. Check History for final transcripts and existing records.
